About this campaign

Take part or check

The recount checks, in your browser and from the public chain, that the campaign kept its promises.

Who can see what

  • Public, on the signed list

    Transaction id, block height and ticket data; the amount in a fundraiser, and every bid once an auction closes. No names, no refund addresses.

  • Sealed to the campaign

    Your refund address, inside the payment message. The host can open every message, so it can see which payments share a refund address.

  • Only on your device

    Your one-time secret and the backup file you download. Relays keep only a sealed copy of your refund ticket.

The public list

Every contribution the campaign has accepted is listed publicly, signed by the campaign.
