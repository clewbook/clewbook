Set up a campaign

Choose what you are running and when it ends. Every contributor gets a signed refund ticket with no expiry height, whatever happens to you afterwards.

MAINNET: real money

This program is set to the real Zcash network

Everything created here runs on Zcash Mainnet, and a mistake can lose real money. Contributors will pay real money into this campaign.

Type

01
Type of campaign

The name is public. Do not put anything private in it. Up to 60 characters, plain text. It is part of the signed rules and cannot be changed later. Without a name, the campaign is shown by a short number.

Terms

02

The most the campaign keeps. With a hard cap, the goal becomes the soft cap: the least it must keep. At the close, payments are counted in the order they reached the chain, and one that does not fit under the hard cap is refunded whole by its own ticket, minus the fee. It is part of the signed rules and cannot be changed later.

How winners pay

The lowest price an item can sell for. A bid must be at least the reserve plus the refund fee to win; if fewer bids reach it than there are items, every winner pays the reserve.

Relays

03, optional

The relays keep the public signed list. Leave this empty to use the ones this program was started with.

What you are about to publish

Your campaign key

Back up the campaign folder

Creating a campaign makes a new wallet and key for it on this computer. They are not sent anywhere. Without them you cannot close the campaign or send refunds, so keep a copy of the campaign's folder (in this program's runs folder) somewhere safe. Contributors can still refund themselves with their tickets.
